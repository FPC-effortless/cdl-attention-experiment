## CSM research continuation

Keep CSM research resumable from GitHub. At each handoff, update the research index with the next experiment, reproducible commands, dependencies, recorded results, and any unresolved dependencies. Commit the source, protocols, required inputs, and concise results on the research branch, push the branch, and verify the remote commit. Use immutable GitHub release assets with SHA-256 checksums for large distributable evidence. For restricted inputs, record their provenance and authorized access path and keep the public replay self-contained wherever possible.
