Final independent consistency review, 2026-11-09

Four specification corrections were requested and incorporated:

1. The identity Delta r_t = Lambda^t Delta r_0 applies within reset-free
   segments of observed tokens. A shared reset makes the difference zero;
   padding does not advance the exponent. The upper bound survives shared
   resets, while the reset-free equality must not be applied across them.

2. The weighted joint contraction norm requires c > 0 in addition to
   c >= K_r rho/(Q-rho), including the K_r=0 case.

3. The training-derivative section now preserves the original controller base
   preactivation C[f; V(cursor)], including its bias. It explicitly identifies
   the standalone gradient checker's simplified, frozen A*f base input.

4. The route-margin bound uses xi for the complete routing input, including
   relevant record values. It does not confuse that input with the token-only
   driver s or imply that a driver bound alone constrains route changes.

limited. This is a recorded review, not a proof of the implementation or a
