CSM-MATH-001 — Independent mathematical review
Scope: proposed token-driven regulator; algebraic verification only
Date: 2026-11-09

VERDICT

The proposed narrow extension has a provably bounded, contractive regulator
when token inputs are held identical. The bounded controller activation does
not imply that the recurrent CSM record update is contractive or stable.
Useful guarantees are conditional record invariance, regulator forgetting,
local route robustness, and explicitly assumed local update stability. No
language-model quality, training success, biological fidelity, novelty, or
wall-clock improvement is established by this review.

The numerical companion verifies these formulas and exhibits bounded
counterexamples. It does not execute or evaluate a trained CSM checkpoint.
An unbounded linear recurrence is NOT evidence of instability of this
bounded-proposal architecture; that argument is explicitly rejected here.

1. FROZEN CANDIDATE AND INDEXING

Let V in R^(S x d) be the record table. Links, cursor, token history and
position retain their original CSM definitions. Let r in R^12 be the added
regulator. Its features f_t are functions only of observed token information,
with no model-generated feedback, record-dependent features, hysteresis or
additional microsteps in the initial candidate. With frozen parameters,

  h_t    = tanh(B f_t + b),
  r_t    = Lambda r_(t-1) + (I - Lambda) h_t,
  u_t    = tanh(C [f_t; V_(t-1)(c)] + U_r r_t).

Lambda is diagonal and fixed for the initial study, with 0 <= lambda_j < 1.
The original CSM heads consume u_t and their original inputs. The existing
read, operation, destination, admission, proposal and write definitions are
otherwise unchanged. A selected admitted write has effective gain

  a_t = epsilon sigmoid(g_t),     0 < epsilon <= 1,
  V_t(d) = (1 - a_t) V_(t-1)(d) + a_t z_t.

Other records remain unchanged. If a separate admission variable is present,
include it in the effective gain. A hard rejected write has a_t = 0. A soft
admission probability introduces an additional derivative term and must not
be omitted from stability calculations.

After processing x_t, the readout predicts x_(t+1). No feature, regulator
input or controller target may use x_(t+1) at that prediction. Causality
follows by induction when every component consumes only its declared
current input and previous state. This is a specification-level statement,
not a verification of an uninspected implementation.

2. REGULATOR BOUND AND FORGETTING: VALID UNCONDITIONAL RESULTS

For finite real inputs, every coordinate of h_t lies in [-1,1]. If
|r_(0,j)| <= R_j with R_j >= 1, then |r_(t,j)| <= R_j for every t.
Proof: r_(t,j) is a convex combination of r_(t-1,j) and h_(t,j).

For two initial regulators exposed to the same token feature sequence,

  r_t - r'_t = Lambda^t (r_0 - r'_0),
  ||r_t - r'_t||_infinity <= rho^t ||r_0 - r'_0||_infinity,
  rho = max_j lambda_j < 1.

This is contraction of the exogenous regulator. The derivative with respect
to record values is zero because the proposed h_t has no record dependence.
The same claim would generally be invalid after adding feedback computed
from predictions or actions: causal feedback is still endogenous feedback.

If the feature sequences differ, the exact coordinate relation is

  delta r_(t,j) = lambda_j^t delta r_(0,j)
    + (1-lambda_j) sum_(k=1)^t lambda_j^(t-k) delta h_(k,j).

Consequently its absolute value is bounded by the corresponding sum of
absolute values. In particular, a persistent feature perturbation bounded
by eta_j contributes at most (1-lambda_j^t) eta_j. Separate this forced
response from the identical-input contraction claim.

3. CONTROLLER BOUND DOES NOT CONTROL ITS GAIN

Every coordinate of u_t lies in [-1,1]. Nevertheless, a large C or U_r can
produce large local sensitivity. For a fixed cursor and fixed f_t,

  D_V u_t = D_tanh C_V E_c,
  D_r(previous) u_t = D_tanh U_r Lambda,

where E_c selects the cursor record and the diagonal D_tanh has entries
between zero and one. Thus, for example,

  ||D_V u_t||_2 <= ||C_V||_2,
  ||D_r(previous) u_t||_2 <= ||U_r||_2 rho.

Bounded activations alone give no useful upper bound below one for these
sensitivities. The original read/proposal heads may introduce further
dependencies and must be included in the complete derivative.

4. VALUE INVARIANCE: CONDITIONAL ON A BOUNDED PROPOSAL

Let K be any convex set. If all initial records lie in K, every selected
proposal z_t lies in K, and 0 <= a_t <= 1, then every record remains in K.
Proof: an updated record is a convex combination of two members of K;
unselected records are unchanged. Induction completes the proof.

For K = [-R,R]^d this gives a coordinate bound. For a norm ball it gives a
norm bound. A tanh-bounded controller does not by itself establish bounded
z_t: the actual proposal function must be checked separately. Epsilon > 1
also invalidates the convex-combination argument in general.

This bounds V and the regulator under the stated assumptions, not literally
every component of the complete state. An unrestricted position counter
grows without bound. Valid cursor and link ranges require their own typed
invariants. Finite floating-point safety also requires excluding NaNs and
infinities; real-number proofs alone do not establish that property.

5. THE GATE JACOBIAN TERM CANNOT BE DROPPED

Fix all discrete choices in a neighborhood. Flatten V into v and let P=E_d
select the written record w=Pv. Define

  F(v,r) = v + P^T a(v,r) [z(v,r) - Pv].

The exact record Jacobian is

  D_v F = I + P^T { a(D_v z-P) + (z-Pv)(gradient_v a)^T }.

The regulator derivative is

  D_r F = P^T { a D_r z + (z-Pv)(gradient_r a)^T }.

The r derivative here can refer either to the updated regulator or the
previous regulator; use the corresponding chain factor Lambda consistently.
For fixed epsilon and no other differentiable admission factor,

  gradient a = epsilon sigmoid(g)(1-sigmoid(g)) gradient g.

These are ordinary local derivatives only while the cursor, links, selected
operation, destination and hard admission choices remain unchanged. A soft
admission factor adds its product-rule term.

Bounded gate counterexample:
  z=1, epsilon=1, a(v)=sigmoid(8v),
  F(v)=(1-a(v))v+a(v),  v in [-1,1].

The interval is invariant, yet F'(0)=1/2+8/4=2.5. Inputs 0 and 0.01 map to
0.5 and 0.5247894467540261, expanding their distance by 2.4789446754.
Dropping the gate derivative incorrectly predicts derivative 0.5 at zero.
This example disproves nonexpansivity; it does not imply that this particular
constant-target system diverges or that a trained CSM has those weights.

A separate bounded-proposal example distinguishes boundedness from local
equilibrium stability:
  F(v)=0.5v+0.5 tanh(3v).
The interval [-1,1] is invariant, zero is a fixed point, and F'(0)=2, so zero
is a repelling equilibrium. This is a constructed member of the bounded
update family, not a measurement of the actual CSM candidate. No unbounded
proposal z=kv is used as a counterexample to the bounded architecture.

6. SUFFICIENT LOCAL STABILITY CRITERION

Consider one active record w and updated regulator q, with all other read
records treated as identical external inputs. Assume a fixed route on a
convex domain. Let

  G(w,q)=(1-a(w,q))w+a(w,q)z(w,q).

Suppose throughout that domain:
  a >= a_min > 0, a <= 1;
  ||D_w z|| <= L_z < 1;
  ||gradient_w a||_dual <= L_a;
  ||z-w|| <= D.

For a vector norm and its induced operator/dual norms,

  ||D_w G|| <= 1-a+a L_z+D L_a
             <= q_w := 1-a_min(1-L_z)+D L_a.

Thus q_w < 1 is a sufficient contraction condition in w for a fixed q.
For the sigmoid gate, L_a <= epsilon L_g/4 when
||gradient_w g||_dual <= L_g. This is sufficient, not necessary. It does
not certify the actual weights without measuring or proving these bounds.

If additionally ||D_q G|| <= K_r and the exogenous regulator contracts with
rho<1, then identical token inputs give the comparison inequalities

  ||delta w_next|| <= q_w ||delta w|| + K_r rho ||delta r||,
  ||delta r_next|| <= rho ||delta r||.

Choose any Q with max(q_w,rho)<Q<1 and c>0 satisfying
  c >= K_r rho/(Q-rho).
Then N(delta w,delta r)=||delta w||+c||delta r|| contracts by at most Q.
Proof: substitute the two inequalities and bound the coefficients by Q and
Qc. This proves stability for the stated active-record subsystem and fixed
route. It is not a strict-contraction theorem for the complete record bank.

The general complete-state Jacobian has the block form
  [[D_v F, D_r F], [0, Lambda]],
because the regulator is exogenous. Every untouched continuous record gives
an identity block in D_v F after rearranging coordinates. Therefore, for
S>1 with at least one untouched record, the local Jacobian has eigenvalue 1
and cannot be a strict contraction in any induced norm. Bounded persistent
memory need not be a globally forgetting system; this is not itself a flaw.

7. HARD ROUTE AND THRESHOLD MARGINS

Suppose routing logits s_i are each L-Lipschitz in a common state/input norm.
At a point with unique winner k and positive margin
  gamma = min_(i!=k) (s_k-s_i),
every perturbation delta with ||delta|| < gamma/(2L) preserves k (for L>0).
Proof: each logit changes by at most L||delta||, so each difference remains
positive. If L=0, logits are locally constant. This is a one-step guarantee;
repeated execution requires margins and perturbation bounds at every step.

For a hard scalar threshold ell > theta, a margin m=|ell-theta| and an
L_ell-Lipschitz logit preserve the decision for ||delta||<m/L_ell. For a
sigmoid probability threshold p, theta=log(p/(1-p)).

Away from ties, a hard-routed update can have a smooth local branch. There
is generally no global Lipschitz guarantee across branch boundaries unless
the branch outputs agree there. For example, write target 1 with gain 0.5
to record 1 when v1>=0 and record 2 otherwise. States (0.001,0) and
(-0.001,0) differ by 0.002 in maximum norm, but their successors differ by
0.5015. A straight-through training estimator would not alter this forward
discontinuity.

8. EXACT OVERWRITE IS A SEMANTIC CONTRACT

For a fixed target z, one update gives
  v_next-z=(1-a)(v-z).
With finite sigmoid logit and epsilon<=1, a<1; an unequal previous vector is
not overwritten exactly. This is a mathematical qualification of the SET
name, not a contradiction of 100% discrete recall in a classification test.
The decoded class can be correct while the underlying vector retains a
residual of its previous value.

For repeated writes toward the same z,
  e_n = product_(k=0)^(n-1)(1-a_k) e_0.
If every a_k>=a_min>0 then ||e_n||<=(1-a_min)^n ||e_0||. If gains vanish
too quickly, convergence to the exact target is not assured. At a=0.5,
an initial error of 1 requires 20 writes to fall below 10^-6. If exact SET
is needed, specify a separate hard assignment or explicit numerical error
contract; do not silently reinterpret the current implementation.

9. DISJOINT DESTINATIONS ARE INSUFFICIENT FOR PARALLEL INDEPENDENCE

Starting with (A,B)=(0,1), consider
  U: A <- 0.5 A + 0.5 B,
  W: B <- 0.5 B + 0.5 A.
U then W gives (0.5,0.75); W then U gives (0.25,0.5); evaluating both
proposals from the original snapshot gives (0.5,0.5). The destinations are
disjoint, but each write changes the other's read input.

Sufficient conditions for commuting isolated operations are disjoint write
sets, no cross read/write dependencies, and no shared control dependencies.
They must also leave the other's routing/admission decisions unchanged.
For two same-destination affine writes with fixed gains a,b and fixed
targets z1,z2, the difference between the two orders is ab(z2-z1), so they
also generally do not commute.

The exogenous regulator is an affine recurrence and can be composed in a
parallel scan. This does not remove the original CSM controller's dependence
on the prior updated record. Algebraic associativity of general transition
functions alone does not provide a compact efficient whole-model scan.

10. TIME UNITS AND INITIALIZATION

The initial model should define time in tokens. A half-life H_j tokens gives
lambda_j=2^(-1/H_j). An alternative relaxation time tau_j gives
lambda_j=exp(-1/tau_j). Physical elapsed-time decay would need
lambda_j(t)=exp(-delta_t/tau_j); it is a different model and can make behavior
depend on serving pauses. Padding should not advance a token clock.

With r_0=0 and constant h=mu, r_t=(I-Lambda^t)mu. A slow trace is therefore
small during a short prefix. That is the specified cold-start behavior,
not proof that the trace estimates a stationary mean without bias. Warming
with the first observation or bias correction changes the model and must
be declared. Resets and session carry-over must follow the same boundaries
in training and evaluation. These choices can affect measured behavior even
when every algebraic bound in this review holds.

DELIVERED CHECKS

verify_stability.py produces verification/stability.json using deterministic
synthetic values. It checks finite-difference derivatives, convex bounds,
regulator forgetting, route margins, noncommuting writes, exact-overwrite
residuals, and one explicitly constructed subsystem satisfying the sufficient
stability criterion. Random/grid checks are numerical corroboration, not
formal proof. No trained checkpoint, dataset, language accuracy, speed
benchmark or Lean compilation is claimed by this independent review.
