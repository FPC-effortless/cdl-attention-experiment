# COG-ATTN-092–095 frozen protocol and diagnostic distinctions

## Non-negotiable boundaries

1. **No gold answer injection.** Training or test-time agents may receive puzzle state, goal and legal transitions (and terminal win/consistency feedback) only. They must not load reference paths, annotated mate moves, exact Sudoku solutions, hidden maze maps or 8-puzzle distance buckets. The benchmark *generator* may use certified solutions to construct tasks; the agent policy must remain isolated.
2. **Credit the real system under test.** COG-066 `acquire()` is a pinned upstream component used in inherited 091 maze trace analysis, while these follow-up policies are new transparent interpretable controllers. Search, MRV and game-rule implementations are explicitly supplied and scored as baselines; we do not claim that an autonomous artificial brain discovered them.
3. **Strict temporal splits.** Maze uses the same ten development training worlds as 091; levels 6–7 development cases for parameter calibration; fresh seeds (9901,9907,9913,9923) are reserved for 092. Sliding 093 trains on 19 development worlds, tests 25 reserved instances, freezes the model, then tests 32 disjoint exact-depth 8-puzzle states at seeds (11003,11027,11057,11071). Sudoku 094 uses 20 development cases to choose a search operator and 20 reserved for outcome. Chess 095 learns from 12 development roots levels 1–6, then evaluates 20 reserved roots with forced mate in 1–4 moves.
4. **Strong matched controls.** Maze: goal-directed DFS heuristic and unconditional local learned policy. Sliding: exact Manhattan A* with identical legal actions, node cap, cache mechanism and an action-rotated learned-preference negative control. Sudoku: identical backtracking engine with first-open, MRV and max-open selection. Chess: identical AND/OR search and strategy validator, with shuffled learned move-preference control.
5. **Complete correctness checks.** Maze records physical forward/backtrack actions; any invalid move fails. Sliding validates final state and exact-path length on separately generated full-distance 8-puzzles. Sudoku requires every row, column and box valid; exhaustion is failure. Chess must win against every Black legal reply within its permitted White move depth. A search budget exhaustion is not success.
6. **Real cost.** Report success first; then primitive attempts, expanded states, and full policy wall time. Include model fitting separately and do not infer total deployment savings from a search-work reduction alone. Any non-parity competence result disqualifies an economic speed claim. A local CPU benefit needs replication and an explicit amortization horizon.

## Decisions made before heldout scoring

092 calibration evaluates threshold in {2,3,4}, skill cutoff fraction in {0.25,0.6,1.0}, online penalty {0,1} on development levels 6–7 for budgets ×1 and ×2. It selects maximum completions, then lower physical action count. **Result:** (threshold=2,fraction=0.6,correction=0). 093 uses source-root quorum >=2, frozen raw feature counts and standard admissible Manhattan A*. 093b changes only how features are calculated and does not retune model parameters. 093c uses the same frozen learned model and exact-depth state generator; depth-2 states were excluded because no sufficient disjoint pool existed. 094 chooses min training failure, then min search nodes for each grid size. 095 admits features with >=2 independent development roots.

## Threshold for future architectural promotion

A complete COG-ATTN model must be evaluated from raw environment observations, discover at least part of its own reusable search/control rules, generalize to new environment families and difficulty levels, recover after interventions, and improve total inference cost at matched outcome quality. This cycle does not pass that broader gate. Never reinterpret these experiments as evidence of zero-shot general intelligence.
