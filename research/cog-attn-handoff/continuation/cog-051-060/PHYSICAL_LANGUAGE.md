# Authentic narrated physical actions

Use EPIC-KITCHENS-100 annotations at upstream commit ea8b40457a400c3fffa1c7f406ef3dc169cc2522. Train only on the official training videos and evaluate the official validation videos. Hold training videos whose SHA-256 ID modulo 7 equals zero aside for calibration. Fit from-scratch character/word bag logistic models for the verb and noun separately and a flat joint-action reference. Use a maximum of 18,000 training rows sampled with seed 5152, and retain every evaluation row. Compare exact verb–noun accuracy, new verb–noun pairs with known factors, unseen people, and a stress slice with literal catalog verb/noun tokens masked using a training-only lexicon. Gate admissions at 99% calibration precision and report recall across every evaluation action. Treat annotation classes as supplied weak grounding. Some labels derive from the narrator's own words. This benchmark measures grounded descriptions of actions in recorded human activity. Video pixels, causal effects, intent and instruction-following require separate evidence.
