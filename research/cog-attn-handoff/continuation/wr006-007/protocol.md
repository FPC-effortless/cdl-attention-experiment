# WR-006 and WR-007 initial pilots

WR-006 compares the WR-003 per-entity ordered valid-time list against a transaction-versioned, path-copied valid-time segment tree. Test eight seeds at 128, 2,048 and 32,768 writes. Each has 512 queries, 75% hot-key sampling, 30% late writes, and independent cutoffs plus a deliberately old transaction cutoff. Require exact equality to full scan. Record query work, time and construction/storage costs. Ties select latest transaction. The pilot uses a known bounded integer valid-time universe, ordered unique transactions, and append-only corrections.

WR-007 tests binary meaning acquisition with known action family and correct entity binding. Reuse the original active informative verification setting and the seven-observation maximum. On an independent calibration split estimate symmetric verification error inside two context classes. Compare the original 0.95 posterior rule with a pooled calibration, a criticality-aware rule with pooled calibration, and a finite-horizon policy with context-specific calibration. The adaptive policy minimizes expected cost by choosing between promotion, another verification, and deferral. Error costs are 20 and 100 for low and high criticality, verification costs 0.15, and deferral costs 1. Require high-criticality wrong admissions to fall and track the cost and coverage of doing so. Promotion risk may cost at most 0.20 in the criticality-aware arms, corresponding to posterior thresholds 0.990 and 0.998; no promotion before a verification. Compare on paired trials at 5% and 15% independent error plus a separate correlated-noise stress. The stress probes model misspecification and is not a qualification gate.
