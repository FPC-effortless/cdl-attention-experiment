The experiment is a constructed test with a supplied entity catalog, a fixed operator family and verb inventory, and explicit supported single mutations. It cannot establish arbitrary-language execution safety. The MiniLM artifact is pinned by revision and bytes. All evaluator labels for role scoring come from exact frozen facts, while ambiguity annotations are authored.

For fully specified NLI holdout commands, also replay a costed clarification policy. Choose one of source, destination or operator by maximum posterior marginal entropy, receive one exact simulated response for that slot, and recompute the highest-scoring compatible action. Compare with no clarification and full explicit confirmation. A response is one resolved slot for the entropy policy and three resolved slots for full confirmation. This diagnostic supplies truthful feedback and measures counts, not round-trip time or availability. Commands without a determinate interpretation remain deferred in this comparison.

The exploratory post-response policy fits one conditional confidence cutoff on the existing clear calibration examples after simulating the single-slot answer. It releases as many calibration answers as possible at 99% exact precision, then applies that same threshold to the implicit and correction cohorts.
