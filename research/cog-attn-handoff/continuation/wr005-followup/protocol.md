# WR-005 residual mass follow-up

Audit final exhaustive and destination-conditioned checkpoints at 32 entities using 256 fresh examples per cohort (familiar, held pairs, recombined, seed offsets 7100–7102). Split changed and no-op transitions. Compare exact and proposed compatible mass and per-example gradient cosine in the concatenated source, destination and operation logits. Report coverage, mass, norm error and cosine, including conditional cosine only where both gradients are nonzero. Average five separately sampled proposals per example. These are checkpoint diagnostics; gradient agreement is local to the parameterization and checkpoint.

Train five arms at 32 entities with fresh seeds 13, 37 and 61 under the same schedule and cohorts as the pilot. Compare exhaustive, fallback-only destination-conditioned, and always-sample residual schemes. Always-sample arms draw 16 uniformly without replacement outside top-8, restricted to the changed destination on single writes and unrestricted on no-ops. Compare unit weighting with inverse inclusion weighting at 16 draws, and inverse inclusion weighting at 32 draws. Evaluate on text only. Use the original per-seed state, changed-shortlist recall, and check-count gates. Track support, checks and wall time.

Top-8 contributions are exact within that subset. The weighted residual estimate has expectation equal to omitted compatible mass at fixed parameters, since each residual action has inclusion probability sample_size / residual_size. Destination restriction excludes only zero contributions on single writes. The logarithm and normalized gradient remain biased, particularly when the estimated mass can be zero. Zero-mass examples are skipped consistently with the pilot. No proposal probability or selection index is differentiated.
