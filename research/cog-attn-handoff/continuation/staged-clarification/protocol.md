# Staged clarification with independent provenance

## Frozen workload

Use the earlier separate-role frozen DeBERTa model to produce 24-way action posteriors. Its ONNX revision and byte digests come from the semantic-transfer run. Induce the four novel spellings for each seed from 96 new support transitions. Use the prior concluded calibration bank for threshold selection and fresh test seeds 751, 773, and 797. Each test seed has 128 implicit single commands, 128 corrections and 128 ambiguous commands. New templates have a mixture of receiving-ledger and reference-ledger cues. Ambiguous commands have no executable gold binding. Their responders may decline or guess. The simulated independent sources may have independent study errors. Every comparison uses the same score bank and pre-generated reply tape.

Four declared reliability profiles: exact replies; independent 5% substitutions and 10% unavailability; a shared wrong snapshot (15% of commands) affecting services 0–3 and declared as a single evidence root; and the same shared snapshot with those services falsely labeled as independent roots. The shared snapshot switches the intended source and destination. Services 4–6 use separate observation roots. Sample wrong answers from alternative labels. Some replies in the imperfect profiles have a visible stale epoch and must be excluded by a guarded policy. On indeterminate commands, honest responders report unresolved; the shared faulty source abstains or guesses according to the fixture. A declaration of source independence can be false.

## Policies

- One slot, unguarded. Ask the maximum-entropy slot and hard-condition the posterior on an answer.
- Two slots, unguarded. Ask source and destination once each and hard-condition.
- Calibrated staged posterior. Use a tempered prior, update under the declared independent 5% substitution model and available provenance, ask up to seven slot questions, and require two different role slots to have at least one valid answer. Tune only a confidence cutoff on the prior clean calibration bank. Include a clustered-provenance version and a version that counts service labels. This estimates a policy under possible misspecified dependencies.
- Provenance quorum. Ask about the maximally uncertain unconfirmed role (source or destination), then ask for a second independent confirmation before accepting it. Require agreement from two distinct evidence roots per role, a lexicon operator probability at least .99, a complete supported action, and no conflict among live quorums. Avoid requesting another reply from a root already observed for that slot. Up to eight attempted fields. Deferral preserves state. Repeat with declared independent service labels to expose the value and limitations of provenance. Missing, stale and unresolved responses provide no vote. An explicit unresolved reply from an in-epoch source quarantines the command in the guarded policies.
- Simulated full verified intent provides the upper bound only. Its truthfulness is a fixture property.

Predeclared qualification in each profile: determinate coverage of at least 70%, exact action precision of at least 99%, and ambiguous admission at most 1%. Also report exact count of wrong admitted actions and erroneous state effects. Zero observed errors does not prove zero population risk. Per-question cost counts attempted fields, including unavailable replies, with no wall time claim. Preserve action and provenance logs. Risk from dependence and wrong clarifier feedback are deliberately simulated, and no real-user evaluation is claimed.
