# WR-004 and WR-005 pilot protocol

Compare three learners with identical raw text features and factorized source, destination and operation heads. Exhaustive training sums the full compatible mass. Top-8 training sums compatible mass in the shortlist and skips rows with no support. Top-8 plus exploration conditionally draws up to 32 distinct actions uniformly without replacement from the complement, adding any compatible draws to the objective. The truncated objective uses global action probabilities, no shortlist renormalization and no importance weights. It is a biased surrogate and finite exploration can still miss all support.

Use seed 11, 29 and 47, 8 and 32 entities, 400 steps of 64 examples, and 1,024 examples per test cohort. Learn from state transitions only. Keep the original copy/add modulo-four executor and held-pair rule from WR-001 (upstream run.py blob 56e3d3ac3c379a646f09e9630fb8f3681c0f4468). Train on two cue styles and two aliases per entity, randomized mention order, and a distractor entity. Test familiar text, held pairs, cue/verb recombinations, unseen verbs and unseen aliases. Freeze the feature vocabulary on training text. The encoder sees only tokenized strings with unigram and adjacent bigram features. States and action labels are unavailable to the encoder.

Report exact action and consequence accuracy, including changed transitions, and shortlist gold/compatible recall. During training report pre/post-exploration support, rescue of missed rows, skipped rows and executor checks. Measure single-process training wall time. Require each seed at both sizes to achieve 95% familiar and 85% held-pair and recombined state accuracy (WR-001 thresholds). Require the bounded exploration arm to clear those gates, reach at least 95% compatible test shortlist recall on changed examples for those three cohorts, and average at most 40 executor checks per training example. Record the two unseen-language controls without a qualification gate. A wall-time speedup is a separate empirical result.

This is a synthetic compositional pilot with a fixed entity universe and known executor. It tests support-aware training before integration with TAC/LRC. Gate thresholds were set before the first training run.

## Follow-up after the initial diagnostic

The first run showed a large 32-entity accuracy drop with uniform residual exploration. Add a fourth arm, transition-conditioned exploration, under the same gates. If a single location changes and top-8 has no compatible action, sample the same bounded number of distinct residual actions restricted to the changed destination. A one-location executor makes this restriction safe for the support of the training objective. Retain uniform residual sampling on no-op transitions. State differences are used exclusively for fallback during training. This arm was designed after seeing the diagnostic and uses the same seeds, so it is a follow-up ablation rather than independent confirmation. The final script also samples complement ranks directly for both exploration arms, avoiding a dense action list in the fallback.
