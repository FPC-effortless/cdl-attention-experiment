## Authentic issue-history diagnostic

The [real GitHub issue-history report](issue-history-study/README.md) covers 2,340 public issues from five repositories. Exact replay agreed on every recorded prefix checkpoint. The learned admission policy fell short of the frozen precision and coverage gate, and the input-matched flat reference ranked better in the held-out repository. The handoff includes frozen recipes, per-comment predictions, source digests, and the replay audit.

The [snapshot reconciliation follow-up](issue-history-followup/README.md) reverified the six known stream omissions and tested the valid-time and knowledge-time boundaries. It also preserves the frozen design for the pending fresh-cohort comparison.

## Download and verify

For a complete original archive, use the bundled [downloader](https://github.com/FPC-effortless/cdl-attention-experiment/tree/ba8777f522db0efa17f855c744993135abe1da2d/download_evidence.py). Small archives have a single file and larger ones are in ordered parts. The downloader verifies each part and the reconstructed original.

```bash
curl -fsSLO 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FPC-effortless/cdl-attention-experiment/ba8777f522db0efa17f855c744993135abe1da2d/download_evidence.py'
python3 download_evidence.py --ref ba8777f522db0efa17f855c744993135abe1da2d --list
python3 download_evidence.py --ref ba8777f522db0efa17f855c744993135abe1da2d --all --output ./verified-evidence
```

The [part manifest](https://github.com/FPC-effortless/cdl-attention-experiment/tree/ba8777f522db0efa17f855c744993135abe1da2d/manifest.json) pins every stored part.

## Integrity

[Evidence manifest](evidence-manifest.json) records the byte length, SHA-256 hash and archive file count of each bundle. [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS) verifies downloaded archives with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. [Source manifest](SOURCE_SHA256SUMS) records the hashes of the browsable copies. Archive directory layouts vary and match the original deliveries. The [upload verification](PUBLICATION_VERIFICATION.json) records a public download and hash check of every part and reconstructed archive.

## Coverage boundary

The parent handoff preserves the reports and protocols for COG-ATTN-001–046, both lifecycle cycles and WR-001–003. Its [archive manifest](../ARCHIVE_SHA256SUMS) lists ten older binary archives. Those archive bytes were unavailable in the publication workspace. Historical reports and the earlier checksums remain on the branch. The separate [LRC frozen repair release](https://github.com/FPC-effortless/cdl-attention-experiment/releases/tag/lrc-route-repair-002-20261108) has the upstream LRC artifacts.

Publication preserves evidence and the qualification status stated in the individual reports. The scoped CPU comparison and typed replacement bridge passed their stated gates. Authentic update recall, full original TAC recovery, independent external authority, accelerator and cross-host qualification remain open.
