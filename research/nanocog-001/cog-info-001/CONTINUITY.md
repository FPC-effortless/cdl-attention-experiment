# COG-INFO sits inside the existing nanoCOG program

COG-INFO-001 is a frozen checkpoint audit of the existing local binding
failure. COG-INFO-002 is a prospective causal follow-up to that evidence.
They do not replace the original COG-CORE work:

1. **Local binding and readout.** Keep the original and matched controls,
   counterfactual cell design, and 95% world plus 90% complete-family
   floor per named seed. Prior unsuccessful ROLE and NAME-CONSIST
   results are retained. Use INFO diagnostics to choose a targeted
   next repair, not to reset the baseline.
2. **Bounded retention and routing.** After local binding is reliable,
   compare learned write/selective retention at fixed capacity against
   equal-budget FIFO and recency controls. The original probability
   survival result is separate from learned role binding; holding this
   stage is not abandoning it.
3. **Language and full inference economics.** At matched quality,
   compare optimized attention and appropriate SSM/COG baselines under
   common data and hardware. Charge prefill, decode, tokenization,
   routing, reads/writes and relevant verification or repair. Keep
   workload-specific wins distinct from broad attention-replacement claims.
4. **Other research lanes.** Prior COG-LM, COG-INTERACT, CSM and LRC
   results and their provenance stay as distinct references, not results
   attributed to nanoCOG. No active-inference controller or scale-up
   assumed necessary until stagewise and causal evidence indicates it.

All exact current evidence remains in draft PR #88; private replay
archives stay in the research Space. No MAIN or protected TEST was
opened by INFO-001.
