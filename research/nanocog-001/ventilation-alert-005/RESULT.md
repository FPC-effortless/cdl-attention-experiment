# VENTILATION-ALERT-005: reading age versus calibration error

**A biased witness hurt paired accuracy more than older readings for every full-history model. No model qualified.** Moving the same observations five ticks earlier cost tied COG 4.05 percentage points on average (descriptive 95% paired interval −9.03 to +0.69); biasing the witness cost 30.21 points (−35.82 to −24.65).

## Design

Twenty-one new CPU fits: seven adapters, seeds 173/271/907, 192 updates per fit, batch 32. Matched features, training draw order and exposure, declared 9,000–13,000 parameters. Not a matched-FLOP comparison. 512 disjoint fit families and 288 held families; every held family is replayed in four conditions. Each family has two worlds with identical first 12 ticks and different correct final alerts. Shift exactly three post-change primary/witness samples together: age 0–1 vs. 5–6 ticks. Hold their values, count and noise fixed. Independently add a signed 5–10 unit offset to the late witness only. Primary readings, visibility and truth are unchanged within that contrast. No latent changes occur after the last sample. This isolates age of available evidence, not forecasting new changes during missing intervals.

Finite-panel input-consistency ceilings are 288/288 for a full sequence and 0/288 for the first 12 ticks in each cell. Last tick alone: 147/288, 143/288, 0/288, 0/288. This is exact sample consistency, not learnability or population accuracy. The last-only MLP cannot solve stale pairs because the last inputs for both worlds are identical. No real telemetry, hardware or control; held labels were detached from the worker.

The correction rule went from 91.67% complete with calibration to 34.03% with offset. Tied SDPA had the highest paired counts in both biased-witness cells, but under-alerted 108/576 severe worlds in the stale/offset cell (18.75%). GRU led the recent/calibrated learned count by one pair and TAC-OSM led stale/calibrated; these are sample ranks, not demonstrated superiority. No arm met the 85% complete and ≤2% severe-under gates in all cells together with the declared minimum differences from the correction rule.
