# COG-BIND-001U: query-independent copying is consistent but does not learn novel item roles reliably

## Findings
When the correct event is supplied after prediction, the event-only reader's span scores are bit-identical under a different question about the same passage. This verifies that the question no longer contaminates the local score. It did **not** make the learned item role robust. On novel wording, correct same-word swap pairs rose from **608 to 666 of 1,536** pooled seed observations. The entire gain was not repeatable: one seed improved, one tied, one declined. All three missed the prospective gate. The familiar and relocated conditions also regressed materially in the weakest seed.

**The most informative failure: every fitted model preferred the wrong item in the unfamiliar active construction with the locative between actor and verb.** In all 1,536 model-row observations (256 underlying rows across six fits), the nearby distractor scored strictly above the true item. Restricting the output after the fact to just the two item tokens recovered **zero** examples in that cell. The defect is misranking the role, not just an unmasked vocabulary or interference from a question. In another seed the two successful novel strata were complementary in a way that yielded zero complete paired-question families, despite 244 exact item-swap pairs.

A read-only audit of the prior 001T stored logits found that even with the scored event held fixed, changing to the other event's question changed the predicted position in 8,126/24,576 direct and 7,094/24,576 event-first observations. 001U addresses that interface dependence, not proof that removing it is sufficient. The 001U post-hoc two-item restriction helped some other forms, but pooled novel native individual accuracy was 1,296→1,382/3,072 and restricted accuracy was only 1,518→1,546/3,072. Literal fixture grammar remains exact. First/last copying reaches 768/1,536 pooled matched swaps and 64/128 complete novel families per seed because both payload positions align in half the families; complete-family counts alone are therefore not shortcut-proof.

## Method
The benchmark supplies one constructed two-event utterance and asks about one of its events. Both arms get the same raw comma-and split, independently score every token position in both events, and receive one matching item outcome per training question. The paired complementary questions provide balanced training of both events; held evaluation uses the externally correct event only after prediction. Both candidate items use the same article and trade roles in exact same-word matched pairs; a simple fixed first/last choice is 50% in each held view. The explicit locator grammar still solves the constructed task. It is a control, not evidence of learned general-language reasoning.

The same 12,927-parameter character-composed convolutional pointer and loss are used with matched initializations, row orders, 384 updates and 49,152 target exposures in each of three seeds. The comparison replaces the query side stream with the raw event itself; it is not just blanking a question. Event-only sees no actual question even when changing it would point to the other event. Both approaches use the same padded shape and number of event computations. Event-only sees more active side tokens, 811,008 vs 540,672 per fit. Neither model has learned per-word embeddings or a candidate mask. A shared spelling inventory includes held item forms, but those exact forms do not occur in training examples. The synthetic provenance, conditioned token exposure and aligned repeated families matter to interpretation. We did not qualify throughput or autonomous routing.

Training, development and held have 256, 64 and 128 semantic families. Each has 8 rows crossing both noun-role assignments, both physical event orders and both target queries. Familiar, relocated and novel held are aligned views of the same 128 families. The pre-registered novel decision uses four target renderer cells (active/passive × two novel word orders), each with 128 matched swap pairs. An inactive v1 was rejected before any fit because a word was seen only in raw training questions and not in event text, which would have disadvantaged the query-free condition. The frozen v2 trains on that word at a known event position, preserves every evaluation byte and leaves the novel placements unseen.

## Decision
Do not adopt this standalone reader as a binding repair. Preserve the useful engineering interface (event extraction independent of the question), but don't mistake invariant predictions for correct predictions. Rather than sweeping another confidence threshold or smoothing this pointer, compare a role-aware local extractor that explicitly binds the action to its object and separates the modifier, on fresh sentences where a literal locative-complement heuristic is not sufficient. Re-test the composed pipeline only when that local rule transfers.

Six matched CPU fits and 24 blind prediction bundles, with no reruns. The read-only predecessor diagnostic and the post-seal two-candidate intervention are not evaluation policy changes. Results apply only to raw events separated by the visible delimiter and selected externally.
