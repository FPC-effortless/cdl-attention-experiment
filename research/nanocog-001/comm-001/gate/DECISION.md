# COG-COMM-001 gate decision policy

Prospective: separate calibration of learning from measurement of literal route availability. No registered main-model fit is admitted here. Neither gate accesses the main CAL or DEV artifacts.

1. The easy construct tests whether the same two-layer local decoder can optimize the same causal LM, outcome, and paired-foil objective when both bindings are actually inside its receptive field. It is not a held-alias, long-context, or authentic-language result. Use both absolute exact world/pair decisions and a case where the two prefixes are different but their final local fields are identical. The latter must leave predictions identical, with zero complete opposing-answer pairs. No acceptance by pooled seed average.
2. The scratch diagnostic runs initialized weights only against the audited TRAIN inputs. Count no publication, publication but not retained at query, retained but not selected, and selected, with all eligible worlds in the denominator. Show exact answer-token and supporting-span counts separately. The observed route positions describe literal evidence opportunity; later hidden vectors can represent older information. This is not an optimizer result, and low initialized support alone does not imply a trained route is impossible.
3. Outcomes govern the next decision. An easy failure stops expansion and points to normalization/objective/tokenizer/optimization; do not attribute main negatives to architecture. If the easy construct passes but scratch loses almost all direct support, run a separable retention intervention on TRAIN before a main comparison (e.g. a raw-input-triggered write policy vs unmodified per-token pressure, with fixed query coverage and no supplied support IDs). If retention is adequate, freeze real-shape resource caps and exact role separation before any matched main experiment. Do not retroactively retune thresholds or recycle a calibration panel as held evidence.

The remaining CPU cap and independent source/outcome audit are requirements, not evidence of capability. Preserve failures, artifact fingerprints and scope restrictions in the written result.
