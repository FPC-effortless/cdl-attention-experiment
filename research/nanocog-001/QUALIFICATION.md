# nanoCOG qualification protocol
**Prospective independent reviewer draft. No model or implementation is admitted by this document.** Separate software parity, mechanism qualification, and language/cost qualification. Preserve COG-PULSE-006 as a distinct negative precedent: ten completed fits, better write addressing with direct current-event access, but only 8/2,400 versus 6/2,400 strict paired answers and 0/600 long-history pairs in either arm. Work must not reuse that exposed panel as fresh evidence or pool it with a new result.

## Claim and architecture boundary

1. **First claim:** on a fresh constructed DEV panel, a stable-identity read interface addresses a distinguishable record without relying on changing record contents or physical row position. The matched no-stable-ID route should fail on a witnessed collision. This is an inference-only, no-fit structural claim, not proof that the language model learns an identity map.
2. **Next claim:** with the same visible history and supervision, a trained model uses its own committed state to answer unfamiliar assignments, preserves unaffected records, and generalizes over declared holdouts better than competent learned controls. Improvements restricted to write/address metrics do not satisfy this claim.
3. **Separate claim:** the same model delivers useful next-token loss and memory accuracy at equal or better complete inference cost. No language competitiveness, general Transformer replacement, or scaling is implied by a small pilot.

Candidate uses no token-to-token self-attention, KV attention, or borrowed SSM. Audit actual source and traced operands, not module names. Initially retain the exact compatible nanochat tokenizer, embedding/output, MLP, residual and normalization, including the upstream untied weights and positional treatment where compatible; document every necessary deviation. Only replace the attention path in the primary contrast. Direct current-event input to the write heads must be separately wired, available at the current event boundary, and not depend on broker selection or a stale stored message. Preserve broker state and current-event source separately in instrumentation. Stable logical IDs must be distinguishable from mutable contents and updating a payload may not implicitly alter its identity. A comparison between one ordinary token driver and one teacher-forced gold-event driver is not an architectural ablation.

## Gate 1 | Falsifiable stable-reader pre-fit held DEV canary

After a separately reviewed data-only freeze, use supplied exact conventional states for a bounded inference-only diagnostic. Pin both a **collision witness** and a **separable positive control** to the real read function, not a mock. Build fresh counterfactual groups with three logical identities, two mutated targets and one unchanged nontrivial third. Construct two states with identical ordered inputs at the tested read boundary after ID removal (verify actual consumed bytes, including cached, positional, recurrent, ordering and mask inputs), and different correct target reads. Invert the placement of the relevant content-to-ID associations while holding the query, payload multiset, validity and unrelated history fixed. Choose cases in which gold target sets are disjoint. On equal-weight opposite sides, any deterministic content-only reader with the same exact consumed input can get at most one of two correct and cannot pass a complete pair. For stochastic readers, fix RNG for the equality check or state the ceiling in expectation under the same output distribution. If the collision cannot be demonstrated through the actual source, report ineligible or refute the hypothesized failure; do not assert architectural impossibility from an approximate or hand-waved collision. Stable-ID implementation must resolve both worlds, not just exhibit distinguishable features. Use a declared exact or calibrated alignment; no best key/row permutation chosen on DEV.

Preregister a fixed roster (suggested ≥96 independent groups balanced across three adversarial strata; final number and support reviewed before materialization); record group IDs and independence. Distinguish stable-ID vs full exact-match, ID masked, stable IDs consistently renamed with query rekeyed, physical permutation with associated keys/values moved together, alias/rekey collision rejection or abstention, and unrelated payload/ID distractor edits. Include chronology reversed in presentation with explicit time held fixed, plus time assignment changed with order held fixed, where timestamp semantics are claimed. Keep valid writes/forced oracle routes diagnostic-only. On this mechanical canary, require **100%** independent exact interpreter/scorer parity, target-pair and collateral correctness from actual untrained deterministic stable lookup on eligible positive fixtures, correct refusal on malformed/unresolvable fixtures, sham bitwise equality, and all witnessed masked collisions meeting their mathematical ceiling; any mismatch is a STOP to fix or narrow the contract, not a prompt to retrain. Report route, record version, payload and answer individually. Passing does not show learned writes, parsing or time generalization.

## Gate 2 | Matched small fits, only after exact pre-fit GO

- Start at roughly 1M only if full accounting with the pinned tokenizer permits. Freeze the lowest feasible common budget rather than shrinking vocabulary or hiding tied/untied weights in one arm. Report total unique registered, trainable, active forward, embedding/output and persistent state separately; account for nontrainable buffers and every extra head. Target ≤1% total and non-embedding parameter distance for learned controls if feasible; otherwise preregister the exact discrepancy and include a capacity-matched comparison before assigning an improvement to mechanism. Never pad with dead parameters. Report absolute parameter counts and the same training and inference precision.
- Mandatory learned references: **unchanged pinned PULSE-006** direct-event and matched-broker lineage where executable; **local-only** matched candidate backbone with memory absent from training and inference; and pinned nanochat self-attention baseline (a control only). Include competent simple recurrent/local-context reference if it is the stronger matched implementation; no imported SSM in the candidate. Treat historical PULSE-006 numbers as prior evidence unless exact source/weights/recipes and comparable shared panel/protocol are recovered. Separate frozen snapshot replay from retraining on new data; do not call substituted source unchanged. A same-checkpoint disabled-memory mask is a causal intervention, not the trained local-only baseline.
- Mandatory deterministic references: exact typed store under supplied state keys is a ceiling only. Add text-facing deterministic parser/exact store and tuned lexical/recency/no-memory controls receiving the actor's information. Disclose handwritten grammar knowledge, state representation and cost. No oracle keys/op/value fields in the learned path; if a parser provides keys, label that as a separate scaffolded arm and give the identical causal fields to matched controls. Parser errors count in the end-to-end task.
- Primary mechanism ablation is a matched 2×2: direct causal event path on/off × stable identity keys on/off. Use identical upstream input visibility, head family, train initialization outside replaced structures, sample order, optimizer and objective, and matched active parameter and input widths. The broker-only comparator must really lack the direct path; a same-size control that smuggles the current event through another branch does not isolate information routing. Report both main effects and interaction, all seeds and failures. If objective variants are needed, use a separate held-out matched comparison of answer-only, write supervision, read supervision, and both, with known exposure/labels and no extra inference information; never explain a compound change as a single effect.
- Use the same tokenizer, training sources, exact per-seed batches/whole-family order and packing, non-padding tokens per stage, supervised targets, horizon distribution, optimizer updates, tuning/search allowance and stopping rule. Train matched models from equal comparable initialization or use separately labeled checkpoint continuations. Match token exposure first; also report actual FLOPs or a scoped measured counter, CPU process time, elapsed time, memory, optimizer/data work, auxiliary-targets and read/write counts. If equal-token arms materially differ in work, add a separately locked work-matched comparison before claiming sample/compute efficiency. Do not adapt budget after looking at held outcomes. Provide power rationale/effective independent groups; same families across seeds are not additional independent examples. Polarity, swap directions, delayed lengths, no-op histories, unmatched queries, incomplete evidence and stable unaffected records should all be supported in TRAIN/CAL and fresh DEV separately.
- Phase FIREWALL: plan/read/gradient-only software smoke → admitted fixed fit → close all model/checkpoint hashes → blind prediction once → independent sealed scoring. Review source parity for no future data (suffix mutation/prefix truncation; same-position event visibility), cold reload and reset (state isolation across batches), full vs chunked state/causal outputs, checks for hidden gold/metadata, exact clocks/seed determinism, and a healthy nonzero finite gradient on every trainable path that is claimed active. A smoke can establish plumbing only. Timeouts stop at envelope, preserve failure. No training permitted until exact stage reviewer approves.

## Causal diagnostics and result gate

Freeze intervention hooks and theory before running: one-factor-at-a-time masks of current event→write, broker→write, write admission, payload proposal, stable-ID key and selected read-value; preserve all remaining features, cache, weight and query. Always use identity/sham and off-site control, wrong-entity and matched distractor, unchanged third, and report rescues **and** collateral errors. Test at least (i) fixed applied write address but learned commit and payload; (ii) fixed read address over the model's actual state; (iii) correct-or-distractor value injected at the same preselected route; (iv) same-state null returns exact outputs; (v) open-loop prior routes fixed versus closed-loop subsequent choices recomputed. Separate wrong-slot eligibility and missing interventions/abstentions from failures, and distinguish an oracle injecting the correct record from evidence that the model wrote it. Keep exact read keys/IDs and storage state separate from textual decoder output. Report label-free candidate ARMs and teacher-forced diagnostic arms separately.

Primary outcome is exact all answers under both assignment orientations **and** unchanged-third correctness; both direction-specific answer scores and gold–foil margins must agree for a behavioral binding claim. Give separately final full-state and intermediate write/commit/value/false-edit counters, selected correct version and payload, coverage/abstention and stale outputs. Interpret scores conditional on explicit eligibility and publish complete unconditional denominators. Suggest prospective mechanism screen on fresh DEV: ≥80% complete groups in *every* seed, exact ≥95% unchanged-third and no-op preservation in each seed, matched stronger-learned-baseline paired lower 95% group/bootstrap bound >0 and practical margin ≥5 pp for superiority, with per-seed estimates and clusters independent of optimizer seeds. For a memory-dependent claim additionally require directional target-specific intervention and a prespecified superiority or acceptable noninferiority margin on unrelated records, not a broad accuracy drop from any damage. Lock exact thresholds, interval/multiple-testing and small-stratum policy before model DEV. Absent a powered comparable evaluation, report diagnostics only. No promotion from a passing oracle, favorable aggregate with a failed critical stratum, or a low-quality faster candidate.

## Complete costs, reports and boundaries

Measure in same release runtime/hardware/thread/precision with paired randomized order, warmups and repeated runs; capture p50/p95 and paired uncertainty across whole sessions. Time complete calls from raw prompt through tokenizer, routing/lookup, prefill, each state read/write/reset/maintenance, answer projection and decoding; separately record cold model load and cold index/tokenizer setup, warm initial empty state, continuation with exact same cache/reuse opportunity, encoding, prefill and decode lengths, and end-to-end queue/report excluded/included fields. Collect persistent model/state/index memory, peak RSS/allocator (method), temporary copies, per-token/per-event operations, training wall/process time including failures and tuning, throughput and matched workload quality. Do not quote an isolated lookup as serving time. Paired cost frontier compares only comparable qualified quality, includes natural-language performance and controlled state results separately, and gives inference precedence consistent with the user's 12:1 priority rather than mixing elapsed seconds and quality losses. If exact hardware utilization/FLOPs or runner quota is unknown mark unknown, not equal/free.
