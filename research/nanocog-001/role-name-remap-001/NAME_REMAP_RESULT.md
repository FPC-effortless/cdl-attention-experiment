# ROLE-NAME-REMAP-001: binding errors follow literal identities on controlled substitutions

The experiment changed the focal name in its statement and in the query,
while preserving values, order, the queried role and the correct answer.
These are 24 matched blocks and 96 fresh joint name/color families
disjoint from 832 prior synthetic families, using the same seven-token
grammar and familiar one-token names. The four fragile names had been
identified on an earlier exposed evaluation. Controls matched TRAIN
family exposure as closely as the deterministic matching rule allowed.
Both model conditions share the same frozen backbone; they are not
independent replications.

| Focal-query stratum | Native: fragile | Native: matched | Tap adapter: fragile | Tap adapter: matched |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Focal named in first clause | 94/96 | 96/96 | 72/96 | 96/96 |
| Focal named in second clause (primary) | 11/96 | 96/96 | 75/96 | 96/96 |

Primary matched robust-minus-fragile contrast: **+88.54 points**
(native; 24-block bootstrap 82.29–93.75) and **+21.88 points**
(adapter; 11.46–33.33). The prespecified recurrence condition passed
for these literal substitutions in both frozen cases. The effect is not
a transferable generalization or a repaired model. Strict all-287 and
conditional two-color success agreed throughout.

In the focal-second stratum, the native paired substitutions recovered
85 errors and introduced none; the adapted condition recovered 21 and
introduced none. In native, three fragile names scored 0/24 when the
second clause was queried; the fourth got 11/24. The names need not be
impossible: all fragile names were nearly perfect as the first target
in the native model. After the learned repair, fragile second targets
improved markedly, but fragile first targets regressed. Queries about
the untouched distractor stayed 94–96/96 per group. These controls make
the binding failure more specific than generic sensitivity to editing
a token or inability to represent the affected color.

The conclusion is **identity-dependent role selection within this
synthetic grammar**, not a human-name finding, a claim that these
identities are special outside this model, nor proof of the internal
cause. The 24 blocks share names/distractors and the interval is
descriptive conditional on the sampled, prior-selected identity sets.
