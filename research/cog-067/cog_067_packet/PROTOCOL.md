## Matched opportunity contract
Each group has 12 ordered task requests and a deadline at 12 *simulated ticks*. Serving one request costs 1 tick. Direct costs 1 extra tick; replay costs 2. Adaptive, fixed EVSI, learned uncertainty, random matched, and query-ignore use exactly two direct and one replay by the end of first eight offers, with only one query per offer. Force remaining quota in final available slots; that mechanism is shared where applicable. These policies therefore handle exactly eight requests and displace four; passive serves twelve, and always ask purchases the best estimated affordable source until the common deadline. All choose actions from the same trained model, except query-ignore purposely bypasses purchased content. Query-ignore and random matched are negative controls, not sole baselines.

Exact-quota policies match each other in source identity, call counts and simulated opportunity cost, isolating allocation/use. Real measured CPU elapsed is separate; credits represent a common planning deadline model and do not translate to dollars or real call delay. Report source-call wall ns and full per-session time (allocation, query, decoding, inference and decision assembly); offline independent scoring is not part of live response time. A displaced request gets no active intervention, and its lost best-vs-no-op utility is counted. UNKNOWN is abstention, not no-op evidence, and also receives no active utility. Count both separately and publish actual coverage. Draw no claimed economic exchange rate from the reward/tick units.
