# COG-LM-003

Test the learned 002 revision bank as a late-acquired language skill on the published jointly trained 31M models. Freeze their sequence processors and tied embeddings, and train the existing language readouts, lookup mixture and optional bank. This isolates the ability of an existing 31M model to acquire this skill. It is a continuation experiment at one training seed.

Use identical revision documents for short and long COG. In short training reset every state at 128 tokens. In long training carry all states through the entire document and backpropagate through the writes. Use two people with opposite assignments, an explicit correction, and an unaffected person. Swap all assigned values and ask about both people. Train on paraphrased updates and both literal and reworded queries, using 64-, 512- and 2,048-token filler spans. Sample natural-text next-token targets from the filler. Separate a context-length effect at fixed objective from direct supervision at the answer. The long unsupervised-answer control omits direct answer supervision and trains only on matched filler targets. In the routing-pressure condition reset local recurrence and the successor table at the start of half the training questions. The complete model operates normally on every assessment. Train COG and Mamba lookup-only controls on the same intact and masked curriculum. Do not reward large activation norms.

Score unseen names, held-out paraphrases and test articles at 256, 1,024, 4,096 and 16,384 tokens. Report literal and paraphrase full-vocabulary top-1, paired flips and whether both entity queries succeed. Intervene on learned reads while retaining write/read costs. Compare natural-text loss and the 002 authentic dialogue revision panel. Report bank read amplitude, measured CPU throughput, parent and checkpoint hashes. Do not select models on test. Scale only if the intact pressure-trained COG improves balanced revised-value recall and authentic state switches over both simpler controls, preserves natural-text prediction and beats the contemporaneous Mamba throughput. An attention comparison requires an attention model trained on the same curriculum.

## Exploratory utilization intervention

After viewing the initial 1,024-token controlled scores, add an explicit 1% lower target for the ratio of the bank-read norm to the pre-read hidden norm at training answer positions. Keep the same route-masking schedule, seed, training documents, four query forms, supervision and optimization steps. Add 0.25 times the squared positive log shortfall. Track whether measured amplitude increases and whether the additional activation carries the answer under a read-mask intervention. This follow-up reuses the controlled test panel and is exploratory.
