# COG-ATTN-065: proposed actionable communication follow-up

Design only; not authorized to train or evaluate from this packet. The same-input receipt template in COG-066 has full information coverage by construction. Do not spend compute trying to beat a ceiling on that schema.

## Candidate question
When a report must be short, can a learned communicator select and express the facts an independently implemented receiver needs to make the correct next move? The receiver has one of several roles (operator, auditor, requester); they receive the generated message and their own visible state but not the sender's hidden receipts. The sender gets the request and actual causal receipts only. Label training targets using a separate interpreter with simulator event access; never pipe gold next action or future facts to the inference model.

Create new synthetic task families beyond COG-066's receipt fixture. Example: success initially observed, authority revoked and then renewed; whether resume, ask for fresh authority, or report completion depends on which receipt is current, which provider authored it, and which action actually executed. Include novel combinations of roles, refusal types, provenance sources and next-step constraints; no family overlap with train. Paired interventions change one relevant provenance/status fact at a time, plus foil pairs that only change irrelevant fields. Seal a development-only panel before fit.

For the closed receipt control, fix the goal and parcel while substituting wrong-parcel, superseded, or dispatch-only receipts in otherwise matched variants. None is authoritative completion. Swap irrelevant evidence with no expected output change and retain a genuine current completion control. Score true completion recall and false completion separately. This expanded control is future 065 work, not part of 066.

## Comparators and token contract
Same input serialization, frozen word tokenizer and deterministic entity binding for all. Maximum 16 output tokens including EOS, each arm's hard encoding consistency enforced, no extra latent channel. Tune on training-only calibration: (1) strong adaptive template using audience and observed evidence, (2) length-constrained structured compressor/ranker trained with the same labels, (3) flat tiny conditional GRU, (4) role-factorized conditional GRU parameter-matched and trained on the same examples/updates. Keep coverage ceiling from full-length structured reports only as a comparator, not qualification. Disallow opaque codes, embedding side channels and learned receiver collusion. Keep reserve output budget if tokenizer changes; preregister actual counts and scope before fitting. Include refusal/referral or escalation as valid outcomes; no actual external messages or actions.

## Evaluation
Primary outcome: verified receiver completion of the correct simulated next action after reading the message. Co-primary: per-item grounded coverage, false completion and unsupported action claims (zero tolerated), and paired counterfactual sensitivity. Parse scope, status, provenance and next step with an independent grammar and blinded review for valid paraphrases; assess misunderstandings rather than style preference. No free-form language, safety deployment, or open-world skill claims.

Compare within paired families and separately for each receiver role, refusal case, and provenance pair. Require a nonempty pre-fit eligibility check: initial calibration must demonstrate budget can bind, typed oracle does not receive inference-visible shortcuts, no per-example ID/split indicators leak goals, and a short natural-language report can bridge at least some target receiver gaps. Main promotion requires positive verified receiver success or actionable-fact coverage vs the best same-input template/compression baseline with paired family interval, zero false-completed/conflicting claims, and no material harm on the other outcome. Timing includes formatting, decoding, parsing, receiver verification and communication-token count. Exclude this follow-up from the 066 run; it needs an independent task generator, receiver, stronger baseline and pre-fit review of exact budgets/fixtures first.
