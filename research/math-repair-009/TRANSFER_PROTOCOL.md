# COG-SEMANTIC-TRANSFER-001 / REPAIR-010A — real-language evidence use

## Scope and hypothesis
Continue after the successful REPAIR-009 equal-exposure language campaign. Test whether language pretraining transfers into reading comprehension and whether answers actually depend on the presented source. Six architectures (GPT, HH, A0, U, UA, AU), three seeds (9001, 9011, 9029), two initialization conditions (exact frozen REPAIR-009 terminal weights or the same architecture initialized from scratch). 36 independent fine-tunes, all weights trainable, no supplied answer span, entity ID, gold plan, retrieval system, or auxiliary symbolic executor at inference. Same initialization seed, examples, optimizer and replay order for paired conditions. Source hashes in PARENT_MODELS.json verify all 18 pretrained model weights. Use original pinned nanoGPT source, not the closeout timing reconstruction. No new architecture or pretrained hyperparameter selection. Test is not an entire downstream gate or a full proof of reasoning. Prior REPAIR-008 controlled state/executor and authorization gates remain separate and unchanged.

## Data and isolation
SQuAD v1.1 human-authored questions, pinned rajpurkar/squad revision 0a3a8b7b57e8578ec40b2d2bb4c75aca1a6d6ce1, train parquet only (87,599 source rows). https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad/tree/0a3a8b7b57e8578ec40b2d2bb4c75aca1a6d6ce1/plain_text ; CC BY-SA 4.0. Do not access official SQuAD validation or any official TEST. Verify original annotated spans. Keep entire source paragraph, never crop around the answer; deterministic inclusion by UTF-8 prefix length 320–704 bytes, nonempty normalized answer 1–63 bytes, newline-free answer. This restriction is needed to compare with the pretrained GPT position limit, and defines the population being tested. Train/development/held groups determined before training by deterministic 80/10/10 normalized article-title hash buckets. Exclude all articles matching the WikiText pretraining development titles and exclude held titles matching WikiText pretraining train titles; remove exact normalized context duplicates crossing titles and duplicate QA tuples. Exact title and context disjointness enforced between downstream splits. Record all exclusions, full source revision/digest, row/title/paragraph counts, panel member hashes, and exact parent corpus manifest. Near-duplicate and pretraining passage contamination cannot be ruled out solely by normalized titles or exact downstream context hashes; context interventions address but do not eliminate this risk. The frozen REPAIR-009 validation and official TEST stay out of training and evaluation tuning. Require >=12,000 eligible train QA, >=500 development and >=500 held, >=20 distinct held titles; if not satisfied STOP and review, no silent relaxation. Held evaluation artifact not downloaded to training workers; no tuning on held data.

## Fixed training
CPU FP32, 4 threads, 4,096 optimizer updates per fit, two stages of 2,048 updates, 16 QA examples/update (four microbatches of four), 65,536 QA presentations/fit via deterministic successive shuffled epochs of all eligible train rows. 4 independent WikiText replay sequences of 768 target bytes/update from the original TRAIN corpus, never the REPAIR-009 dev/validation, matching order across architectures and initializations. 12,582,912 replay targets/fit. Loss: mean answer-byte NLL including terminating newline + 0.25 × mean replay-byte NLL. For native arms, every training sequence uses three 256-byte chunks with actual recurrent state or KV state carried differentiably across boundaries, no detach, re-encoding of typed fields or gold addresses. GPT processes the identical sequence in one causal forward pass (original positional table). Right padding is masked from the loss. All trainable weights updated; AdamW betas (.9,.95), matrix decay .1, vectors zero, clip1, 128-step warmup, peak 3e-4, cosine to 3e-5. Same schedule for fresh/pretrained, a fixed shared recipe not individually tuned. QA examples and answer target counts reported separately from replay targets; padded bytes are not counted as answer targets. Parent optimizer not carried into fine-tuning. Save source/data/order/parent hashes, RNG, optimizer, model and curve at fixed checkpoints, resume only exact successful boundary; timeout retains partial with zero terminal credit. Production-shape preflight compares chunked vs ordinary forward/backward and verifies nonzero finite gradient through carried native state, not a scientific fit. Stage terminal development teacher-forced QA and full original language dev BPB, no early stopping or selection.

## Frozen held comparison
After all 36 terminal fits, evaluate every eligible held question with greedy generation until newline or 64 bytes, no gold answer length, score normalized exact match and token F1 and teacher-forced answer loss. Single original annotated answer used; not official SQuAD leaderboard. Compare with length-matched irrelevant paragraph from another held article, same question; select absent exact answer substring where possible, record collisions and retain them. Wrong-source control tests source dependence, not guaranteed semantic contradiction or causal proof of correct binding; report both answer-score change and gold conditional log-probability change. Analyze all three seed-wise paired pretrained/fresh differences, source-control gains and baseline competence, article-level uncertainty conditional on seeds. Primary falsifier: failure to improve held free-answer quality over same-architecture fresh condition and/or failure to use correct evidence. No claim of general comprehension if free-answer competence is poor. Require original language-dev regression <=0.05 BPB for continued weights before any promotion. No authority/scope, noncommutative, long-history training or open-world qualification claim in this lane; follow with distinct preregistered cross-state long-history and authorization campaigns. No 10M launch, deployment, merging, paid compute or protected TEST access.
