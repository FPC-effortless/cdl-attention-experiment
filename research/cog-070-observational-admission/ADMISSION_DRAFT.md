## Three claims, three separate gates

1. **Authentic archival replay.** A newly inventoried historically approved development capture can test prediction of real agency-reported observations. Accept only eligible `Approved` target observations, preserving flags and versions. [USGS availability guidance](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/iv-data-availability-statement/) explains the review/approval boundary. Chronological prefixes reconstructed from the approved archive lack original arrival/revision times; the API's `last_modified` is a database refresh, not first publication. Never call this real-time replay or a prospective claim. Do not use eventual approval, qualifiers, outcome coverage, future values or availability as a model feature. Do not treat archive coverage as endorsement of measurement truth.
2. **Prospective read-only field shadow.** Precompute and seal prediction records before target readings exist or are available to the candidate. Keep separate phenomenon time, timestamp available-to-candidate, collector capture time, official quality/approval time and revisions. Independent evaluator outside the candidate operator/team must use their own controlled host, independently obtain originating official observations, custody raw data and keys, and run a separate frozen scorer blind to candidate identities. Before the evaluation clock starts, bind exact station/time schedule, candidate/control versions, verifier code and timestamp/signature procedure. External observed values plus blind independent custody support an independently verified *reported-observation* outcome, not causality. A second endpoint or later USGS `Approved` flag is the same source family; it is not a second physical measurement. Same-operator second clone, same-agent audit, and hosted rerun are reproducibility, not independent-team replication. A separate team must independently execute a fresh preregistered collection/window for that stronger claim.
3. **Verified interactive outcome or external action.** Neither replay nor shadow proves that an intervention caused an outcome. Admission requires a genuine opted-in workflow owner, a narrowly authorized harmless live operation with appropriate control (no physical actuator / consequential decision in this proposal), an outcome recorded by an independently controlled system, safe rollback/failure criteria, and prospective evidence linking the operation to that outcome. No such workflow, independent operator, credentials or authority were identified. Do not substitute mock sensors, synthetic outcomes, retrospective issue confirmation, public mirrors, or a staging write with no operational purpose.
