# LRR-009 — equal-exposure training order and relational retention

Woyengi Intelligence Labs · 6 November 2026

**Decision: do not adopt the tested clean-first schedule.** Interleaved training achieved **85.9375%** on the primary unfamiliar-filler test, clean-first **68.7500%**, and augmentation-first **74.6875%**. Clean-first minus interleaved was **-17.1875 percentage points**. All three prospective clean-first promotion gates failed.

**Important distinction:** the three clean-first models that qualified before augmentation retained **384/384 previously correct DEV answers**. The other two warm-ups never qualified. This is failure to establish a reliable initial relational representation, not evidence that augmentation destroyed already-learned regular answers in qualified models. Do not discard the failed warm-ups or claim that all qualified-start adaptation is disproven.

## Matched experiment

Five fresh initialization seeds **71–75**, three schedules, **15 fits**, each **4,748 parameters**. There are no new inference parameters or losses. The inherited two-sided relational objective and hard indexed runtime are unchanged; the rejected LRR-008 consistency loss is not used.

Per seed, a fixed bank contains **500 minibatches**, eight histories per minibatch, three supervised questions/history, two views/question: 48 question views. Each schedule uses every bank minibatch once clean and once augmented. Every model therefore receives **1,000 AdamW updates, 500 clean events, 500 augmented events, 48,000 question-view exposures and 4,000 unique source histories**. Repeated histories are intentionally not counted as new independent examples.

- **Interleaved control:** clean(0), augmented(0), clean(1), augmented(1), and so on.
- **Clean-first primary:** clean(0..499), then augmented(0..499).
- **Augmentation-first reverse control:** augmented(0..499), then clean(0..499).

All three share initial weights, the exact complete multiset of input/label/perturbation events and optimizer settings. Order changes; AdamW history carries continuously with no reset, extra extension or checkpoint selection. lr=.004, clip norm=2. Temporal curriculum and its resulting optimizer trajectory are jointly changed and cannot be separated by this study.

The generator identifies safe filler positions for training augmentation. This is privileged TRAIN supervision, not autonomous irrelevance discovery. No mask enters inference. Literal entity identity, restricted punctuation-delimited grammar, known predicate vocabulary and eight supplied relation-code slots remain priors.

TRAIN/DEV/TEST retain the audited LRR-008 pair restrictions: all six protected directed alias pairs excluded from TRAIN, DEV=(2,1), TEST=(1,3). Histories and initialization seeds are fresh; the finite grammar and task family are not new external benchmarks. All 15 fits finish before trained-model TEST evaluation begins. Historical LRR-008 percentages are not matched comparators because exposure and initialization differ.

|---:|---:|:---:|---:|---:|

Only **3/5** qualified (required4/5), but those retained **384/384** correct answers. Even the two unqualified models lost none of their few initially correct answers. The bottleneck is reliable initial learning, not demonstrated forgetting of correct regular examples during augmentation. Qualified-start adaptation itself remains supported only within this finite DEV subset; insufficient warm-up qualification prevents a robust promotion claim.

## Generalization and hard-address diagnosis

| H=32 endpoint | Interleaved | Clean-first | Augmentation-first |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Familiar filler words | 87.81% | 71.25% | **94.38%** |
| Untrained in-vocabulary fillers | **85.94%** | 68.75% | 74.69% |
| Novel surfaces mapped to UNK | **30.00%** | 19.69% | 24.38% |
| Additional familiar filler positions | 84.06% | 70.63% | **89.06%** |

Primary recall at H=7/32/128 was interleaved **85.63/85.94/82.50%**, clean-first **68.75/68.75/62.19%**, reverse **78.75/74.69/69.06%**. The grammar-aware symbolic parser/union-find scored **100% in every condition**. It receives a supplied grammar/lexicon; no neural superiority is established.

Clean-first seed72 used **one hard query relation code for all 64 regular H32 queries**, producing 11 correct answers and53 overwrites. Its warm-up was already weak (10/128 DEV), so later collapse is not loss from a previously qualified model. The same initialization and complete training-event multiset reached64/64 regular recall under the other two schedules.

Seed75 used two query codes under interleaved/clean-first, producing25/64 correct and39 overwrites. Reverse order reached64/64 regular recall with four codes, but only36/64 under unfamiliar filler substitution. Conversely seed74 lost regular recall under the reverse schedule. Thus no schedule solved all seeds and generalization conditions, and code occupancy is not a sufficient semantic-success metric.

Unknown filler surfaces share an UNK embedding with opaque values. Their poor transfer remains a representation/context challenge, separate from the primary untrained-but-distinct vocabulary entries. No coreference, novel semantic-family induction, learned code-cardinality or unrestricted syntax is tested.

## Gates

- **G0 integrity PASS.** Exact input-event multiset and initialization parity, fixed model/loss, protected pairs, continuous optimizer and all fits before TEST.
- **G1 capability FAIL.** Clean-first primary68.75% (<80%);3/5 >=70% (required4/5); regular71.25% (<90%).
- **G2 schedule gain FAIL.** Mean-17.19pp rather than >=+10pp; seed72 regression exceeds5pp.
- **G3 qualified retention NOT ESTABLISHED / composite FAIL.** Only3/5 qualified. Retention among those qualified was itself perfect on the measured384 DEV answers.

Retain interleaved augmentation as the better default among these tested schedules, not a universal optimum. Next research should target reliably establishing distinct correct relation addresses, not assume an unqualified clean phase is a sound foundation. No further adaptive schedule or new model was trained after inspecting TEST.

Abstention remains descriptive-only, not a gate for a router not explicitly trained for rejection. No qualified Transformer/SSM language-model comparison, GPU benchmark or general attention replacement was performed. Hard inference avoids query-to-history scoring; the training objective still uses dense position compatibility and answer-token labels.
