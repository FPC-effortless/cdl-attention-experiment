# LRC-DIAG-001: person-key collapse and a failed training repair

Completed 8 November 2026. This is a diagnosis of the recovered sentence-level LRC/VB-001 comparison and a new exploratory nine-fit repair pilot. It is distinct from the raw-byte Tiny Shakespeare study.

## Established mechanism

The three original agreement checkpoints assign all four people's location records in an era to one hard address. Their query/bridge-only page and slot heads cannot observe the shuffled order needed to resolve these collisions. A within-era reversal preserves first-hop actions while changing the required ordinal; no changed-rank case is correct in both orders.

Supplying only the correct second support gives 100% answer accuracy in all three original checkpoints, with fixed weights. Supplying only the first support leaves accuracy near 40–48%. These are privileged diagnostics, not learned capability. Ordinary accuracy follows exactly `0.25 + 0.75 * second_support_recall`; observed mean43.6523% is close to the43.75% expectation from uninformed selection among four location records.
