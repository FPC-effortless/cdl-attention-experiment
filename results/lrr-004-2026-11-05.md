## Open-set rejection, absent facts, and nonlocal phrase failures

At primary H=32 across five seeds × 64 cases each:

| Mechanism / challenge | Control | Locality |
|---|---:|---:|
| Raw model falsely answers unfamiliar predicate | 197/320 = 61.56% | **296/320 = 92.50%** |
| Vocabulary-support guard falsely answers unfamiliar predicate | 0/320 = 0.00% | **0/320 = 0.00%** |
| Guarded positive recall@1 | 65.94% | **80.00%**, identical to unguarded positives |
| Known predicate but no matching entity/relation fact: false answers | 40/320 = 12.50% | **38/320 = 11.88%** |
| Unseen adverb between predicate and value: correct retrieval | 0/320 = 0.00% | **0/320 = 0.00%** |

The guard checks whether the *surface query-predicate position* is in the training query-word lexicon. It cannot generalize to new synonyms and cannot detect a known predicate whose relation is absent; it is a zero-learned-parameter closed-set filter. The known-but-absent target ≤10% is missed. The OOD adverb challenge invalidates any claim that the new model has discovered a flexible, genuinely position-independent relation reader.

A key negative result: **locality increased raw unsupported false answers** from 61.56% to 92.50%, so the supervised recall gain came with worse ungated open-set behavior. A safe production-style setting would need a separately qualified semantic abstention mechanism rather than a vocabulary whitelist.

## Scientific interpretation and next intervention

- Hypothesis supported only in a **narrow optimization-intervention sense**: a soft shorter-address prior improved one pathological basin under five controlled seeds while not causing >5pp harm on any seed.
- Unsolved: robust generalization across seeds, known-but-unanswered questions, syntax variability, learned entity/coreference, unknown relation semantics, ability without known four-code ontology and competitive trained attention/model-level performance.
- Future work should decouple **semantic equivalence / entity-role induction** from nearby slot position, use counterfactual shifts in syntax during training, calibrate answerability under known words but no matching fact, and qualify a stronger attention retrieval baseline.
- The cached retrieval mechanism from LRR-003B remains a separate valid efficiency result. It is not an accuracy repair and no new GPU throughput was measured.

**Decision:** retain both original and locality checkpoints and failed seeds; leave draft PR #40 unmerged, and use this evidence to design the next falsifier rather than immediately increasing parameters.
