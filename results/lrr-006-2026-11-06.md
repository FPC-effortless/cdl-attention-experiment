# LRR-006 — Frozen explicitly trained energy-selection result (2026-11-06)

**Scientific verdict: VALID controlled training-and-evaluation; registered energy-superiority gate FAIL.** Explicitly training an energy readout slightly improves empirical filtering relative to an already-strong scalar threshold, but does not improve hard retrieval, fix weak seeds or generalize under new filler vocabulary. No universal mathematical abstention guarantee, neural semantic superiority, language-model attention replacement or GPU efficiency claim.

## Inference diagnosis independent of EBM

Before this fit, direct frozen-checkpoint diagnostics on the **already reported** LRR-005 H=32 test histories compared original writes, history-only writes (exclude question prefix) and *evaluator-only oracle value-token writes*; all three routes gave exactly the same positive retrieval recall and known-absent false-answer rates in every seed.

For seed 43, all three achieved **7.8125%** recall with **34.375%** known-absent false answers. Even privileged value-only writers did not fix its entity/relation address collisions. Seed42 produced an average **33 non-value writes per history**, neutral to its one currently evaluated question but costly in state. These are **post-result mechanistic diagnostics** and not independently new confirmation tasks.

The address-construction failure is therefore separate from selective answer calibration. EBM training cannot recover a token that the original hard index did not retrieve.

## Mathematical interpretation and decision

A learned EBM aims for `E_valid+margin < E_null` on supervised positive/negative examples. It cannot guarantee that inequality on arbitrary unseen examples. A structural index may guarantee **abstention when no address exists**, but cannot guarantee that learned addresses are semantically correct. Conditional finite-sample guarantees would require additional exchangeability/calibration assumptions and new registered tests.

**Decision:** preserve this experiment as a valid explicit selective answering negative/weak-positive result. Keep PR #42 draft and unmerged. Do not allow the EBM score to substitute for retriever recall@1 or a semantic-address success gate. The next attention-replacement work should target *joint positive same-relation alignment plus negative different-relation separation and collision-control credit* without leaking complete alias classes; then test against a good symbolic and a truly capability-qualified attention comparator at matched training/inference cost.
