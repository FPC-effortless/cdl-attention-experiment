# LRR-008 — nuisance augmentation versus causal-state consistency

6 November 2026. **Primary verdict: G1 incremental invariance FAIL; G2 useful capability FAIL.** Do not promote the additional consistency loss. Augmentation alone improved the primary endpoint in this controlled cohort, but neither treatment is a general attention replacement.

## Registered comparison

A: identical clean views, LRR-007-style two-sided relational supervision.
B: same objective and inputs, but second-view training filler embeddings replaced by Gaussian vectors with random scale 0.5–1.5.
C: identical augmentation to B, plus 0.5 times the mean aligned entity-state, relation-state and gate discrepancy.

**Privilege:** a synthetic generator identifies which training filler positions may safely be perturbed. This is supervised invariance construction. It is not autonomous discovery of irrelevant language. No filler mask or gold role/position is passed to hard inference. The actual inference routine and parameter count are unchanged; dense position compatibility still exists during training.

TRAIN excludes six ordered alias pairings in every anchor/positive/negative question. New TEST=(1,3), DEV=(2,1); all predicate words are otherwise known. Every counterfactual label selects the latest valid record for its entity/relation key. This latest-record policy, new seeds, stricter pair split and independent-history count differ from LRR-007; do not compare its historical 100% directly to these scores. Only A/B/C here are matched.

## Primary H=32, 64 held-out histories per seed

The endpoint replaces familiar filler words at exactly the same positions with quickly/often/truly, whose vocabulary entries received no training examples in this run.

| Seed | A clean | B augmentation | C consistency | C−B |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 61 | 78.1250% | 96.8750% | 60.9375% | −35.9375pp |
| 62 | 98.4375% | 100.0000% | 100.0000% | 0 |
| 63 | 31.2500% | 67.1875% | 67.1875% | 0 |
| 64 | 32.8125% | 43.7500% | 67.1875% | +23.4375pp |
| 65 | 1.5625% | 9.3750% | 9.3750% | 0 |
| **Mean** | **48.4375%** | **63.4375%** | **60.9375%** | **−2.5000pp** |

G1 fails: C does not beat B by 10pp and one seed regresses more than 5pp. G2 fails: C reaches >=70% in only 1/5 seeds; mean primary=60.94% versus >=80% required; mean regular=61.25% versus >=90% required.

B−A is +15pp, positive on all five seeds (secondary finding). Posthoc seed-level descriptive bootstrap intervals: B−A approximately +5.94 to +25.94pp; C−B approximately −21.56 to +14.06pp. Five trained model seeds, not thousands of question rows, are the independent initialization units. Do not turn these intervals into an unqualified population-level superiority claim.

## Stability is not correctness

| H=32 condition | A clean | B augmentation | C consistency |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Familiar fillers | 62.50% | 64.06% | 61.25% |
| Familiar filler permutation | 62.50% | 64.06% | 61.25% |
| Untrained vocabulary entries (primary) | 48.44% | 63.44% | 60.94% |
| New filler surfaces mapped to UNK | 11.25% | 26.25% | 13.75% |
| Extra familiar filler positions | 60.94% | 62.81% | 56.56% |

On the primary substitution, predictions (including empty lookups) remain unchanged relative to the regular case in **99.06% of B cases** and **99.38% of C cases**. C's correctness is nevertheless only 60.94%: consistency can preserve the wrong answer.

At H=7/32/128, primary C recall is 70.94% / 60.94% / 55.31%, versus B 68.75% / 63.44% / 55.94%. The grammar-aware zero-parameter symbolic parser/union-find achieves **100% in every cell**, but receives a predicate lexicon and clause parser. No neural advantage over it is demonstrated.

OOV fillers map to the same UNK embedding as opaque value tokens; this remains a representation/context challenge, not proof about arbitrary unseen-word semantics. Untrained-in-vocabulary and OOV endpoints must not be pooled.

## Post-result frozen-model diagnosis

A separate audit script reloaded every checkpoint and used the unchanged source hard route. On H=32 regular cases, hard recall vs the evaluator-only dense soft training readout was:

| Arm | Hard dictionary inference | Dense soft diagnostic |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 62.50% | 61.88% |
| B | 64.06% | 63.75% |
| C | 61.25% | 65.00% |

Dense diagnostic scoring is not a proposed attention-free runtime. It shows that simply relaxing hard selection does not recover high accuracy: some learned representations are already poor.

Seed 65 assigns the same most-probable relation code to all 64 regular queries in all three arms. C answers 6/64 correctly, with 56 overwrite errors and 2 empty lookups. In seed 61, C's query-code occupancy falls from four codes under B to three; regular recall falls from 100% to 62.5%. These are concrete collapsed-address observations, not proof that every consistency method must collapse.

Known-absent and unfamiliar-predicate false candidates were retained only as diagnostics. **No abstention gate was used for a router not explicitly taught that capability.**

## Audit and decision

- All 225 cells recomputed from 14,400 saved per-case records.
- All 15 model/optimizer checkpoints finite, hashes verified, 4,748 model parameters, all AdamW step counters=500.
- Initial-weight/input/noise stream hashes match paired arms.
- 960/960 regular H=32 predictions replayed through the unmodified source hard inference routine.
- Source/config hashes unchanged from the pre-fit freeze.
- The 14,400 records reuse 960 underlying fact histories across arms/transformations; they are not 14,400 independent replications.
- The audit is a separate program by the same investigator, **not an independent reviewer or external replication**.

Retain augmentation B as a candidate; do not adopt C as default. A staged retention-controlled experiment starting from a qualified relational checkpoint is a possible successor, not something executed here. Unknown-symbol representation and a capable attention/SSM benchmark remain unresolved.

No full LM loss, trained coreference, new semantic-family discovery, inferred relation-code count, GPU throughput or attention replacement is established. PR stays draft/unmerged.

Reproduce: `python -m pytest -q relational_router/test_lrr008.py`; then `python -m relational_router.run_lrr008 --output evidence/reproduction --seeds 61,62,63,64,65 --steps 500 --trials 64`. Exact numeric replay on different hardware/software is not assumed.
