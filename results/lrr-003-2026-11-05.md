- Small 1-layer causal Transformer pointer trained on identical answer-position supervision: **1/96** pooled. It was not independently optimized to reach a valid capability floor and is NOT evidence of self-attention inferiority. Improve the comparator before invoking architecture-performance ranking.

## Efficiency boundary
- Local convolution and index ingest both process sequence tokens; the current convenience route(text) rebuilds the index every query. Total single-query cost is therefore not O(1).
- Hard address lookup does not compute token-pair self-attention. An independently maintained cache is needed for repeated-query cost proof.
- Training wall time: LRR 13.76/12.18/12.14 seconds, Transformer 8.07/7.94/7.92 seconds across seeds. In this CPU training configuration, LRR trains **slower**; do not claim training-speed superiority.
- The two training objectives differ (LRR relaxed address ranking, Transformer dense pointer cross-entropy), despite shared training data/update counts/near-matched parameters. Attention train loss 1.68/1.81/1.12 may indicate comparator capacity or optimization limitations. Further baseline calibration is required.

## Decision
Preserve original result and all six checkpoints. Do NOT merge LRR-003 into the language-model backbone or publicize an attention replacement. The correct next discriminating experiments are (1) streaming cached state to measure actual query cost, (2) robust hard-address/code training with new seeds and a fresh held-out split, (3) unknown-relation abstention, and (4) a capable Transformer or other strong trained retrieval baseline on meaningful text with multiple semantics.

Exploratory seeds 0–2 and variants used to select the local role-offset design are not included in the confirmatory claims.
