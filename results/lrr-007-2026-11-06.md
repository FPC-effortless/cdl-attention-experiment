## Math/architecture interpretation and remaining blockers

**A** optimized `L_ans+0.2[-log(1-s_different)]`; **B** added `0.2[-log(s_same)]`. Both operate on soft relation registers during TRAIN, derived from explicitly labeled answer positions, not from a Transformer self-attention matrix. Inference uses hard lexical role-state updates and a single expected O(1) hash address lookup after O(N) causal input encoding and history writes.

The strong B result supports the hypothesis that **both same-answer attraction and different-answer repulsion are necessary in this implementation's supervised training regime** for stable hard aliases under the specified known lexical grammar. The C margin arm is an informative negative control: relative triplet ranking alone does not necessarily force sufficiently clean discrete address separation for collision-free retrieval.

Remaining: zero novel semantic-class induction, no inferred number of relation codes, literal surface entity equality (no pronoun/coreference), no real unsegmented natural-language syntax, poor unseen-filler transfer, superfluous writes, no trained natural-language LM quality/perplexity, no capability-qualified Transformer/SSM baseline, no measured end-to-end GPU speed or memory traffic. The observed perfect synthetic score must not be generalized.
