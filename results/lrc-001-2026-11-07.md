# LRC-001: implementation passes; relational competence unestablished

Date: 2026-11-07. **ENGINEERING PILOT ONLY. No promotion, superiority or efficiency claim.**

Nine paired CPU fits: three variants × seeds 101, 102, 103. Each has 33,061 allocated parameters, 20 updates, batch size 2, two layers, two reads, cap two. No TEST data was opened. The preregistered engineering protocol is [EXPERIMENT.md](../relational_composition/EXPERIMENT.md).

## Compute accounting

The final pilot executed 360 full training forwards (180 base + 180 counterfactual). Total measured training time was 3.015847s on one CPU thread. This excludes initialization, checkpoint I/O, evaluation, integrity tests and the earlier engineering repeat; it is not a model throughput comparison.

`omitted` sums eligible records excluded by the cap across queries, not unique lost records. Reference dereferences are additional to the two indexed operand reads. Candidate budget is an architectural constraint, not a claim of constant total lookup latency. Each index lookup also uses binary predecessor search.

Both raw manifests/checkpoint sets are retained; the earlier run adds another 360 full training forwards and 2.922585s measured training time.
- Three additional tiny CLI resume checks (2 updates, 2 resumed updates, 4 uninterrupted updates) were engineering-only; they used seed 121 and are not scientific evidence.
- Review, implementation and audit were performed by the same assistant. This is not independent scientific review.

## Next decision

Keep as a draft implementation. Before longer qualification runs, add an oracle-routing competence diagnostic and a separately trained local-only baseline. Then develop the routing objective using DEV only and preregister fresh confirmatory seeds/worlds. Natural text, free-running answers, semantic routing diagnostics, optimized caches and qualified attention/SSM controls remain unimplemented. Do not enlarge the architecture on the strength of this pilot.
